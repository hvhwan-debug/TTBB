Transparency & accountability

The Fund's work is closely supervised in accordance with Vietnamese law.

Clear legal status

The Fund has its own legal status, official seal and bank account, and operates under an approved charter.

Regular reporting

Each year the Fund submits organisational, activity and financial reports as required by law.

Inspection & oversight

The Fund is subject to inspection by the competent state authorities and oversight by its Supervisory Board.

Funds used as intended

All money and assets raised must be used for their intended purpose and beneficiaries, economically and effectively.
