Our Activities

How the Fund supports children living with congenital heart disease.

Congenital heart screening

Working with health facilities to organise check-ups and screenings that detect congenital heart defects early in children from disadvantaged areas of QN province.

Supporting patients' families

Connecting the families of children with congenital heart disease to the right hospitals and surgery programmes.

Medical & humanitarian partnerships

Bridging hospitals and humanitarian organisations in Vietnam and abroad with children who need help.

Raising awareness

Sharing information and the stories of children's journeys through heart surgery to raise community awareness of congenital heart disease.
