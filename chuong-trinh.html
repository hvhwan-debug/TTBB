Our Programme

Congenital heart surgery for disadvantaged children

Many children in the disadvantaged areas of QN are born with congenital heart defects, yet their families cannot afford timely surgery. The Fund stands beside these families, working with hospitals and generous donors to help cover the cost of heart operations.

Every resource of the Fund is used for its intended purpose and beneficiaries, in line with the Fund's charter and Vietnamese regulations on the financial management of social and charitable funds.

  • Accepting donations designated for a specific purpose or recipient
  • Delivering support exactly as authorised by individual and organisational donors
  • Publicly reporting income and expenditure as required by law
