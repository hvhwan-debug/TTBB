About the Fund

Born from the kindness of people in QN who love literature and children.

For Little Hearts Fund (Vietnamese: Quỹ “Vì những trái tim bé bỏng”) was founded by members of the QN Provincial Association of Literature and Arts. Its establishment licence and charter were approved under Decision No. 315/UBND.

The Fund is a charitable, non-governmental and non-profit organisation with its own legal status, official seal and bank account. It works with agencies, organisations, individuals and humanitarian groups in Vietnam and abroad to support congenital heart surgery for disadvantaged children across QN province.
